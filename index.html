Electro Man

Collect three disks on each level to open its exit. The game is saved at every checkpoint.

← →walk
↑jump
↓use a teleport or the exit (standing on it)
Space, Enterfire (needs a battery)
Tshow / hide the touch buttons
Msound on / off
Escmenu
Shift+arrowslook at the next screen (debug)
Rrestart the level
[  ]previous / next level
Nfinish the level now (debug)
Ffullscreen
Apixel shape: square / 4:3 monitor
P  .pause / one tick
−  =ticks per second
Hshow / hide this panel

Install it as an app to play full screen and offline: in Chrome, "Install app" in this game's menu or in the browser's menu; on iPhone and iPad, Safari's Share button, then "Add to Home Screen".
